The author agreed to them being stored here under the repository's MIT license, with this credit:
https://github.com/kumarganduri/aimpg/discussions/2#discussioncomment-19628758

`worker.md` is used as posted, without changes.

`planner.md` has three changes, made after pilot 1 in the author's own wording:
https://github.com/kumarganduri/aimpg/discussions/2#discussioncomment-19632627
1. Opening paragraph, last sentence: the worker always writes the first version of the change; the planner may make a small repair only after the worker has reported. (In pilot 1 the planner made both changes itself.)
2. Section 5, first bullet: the same rule, stated where the small-fix exception is.
3. Section 6: if the planner had to choose one reading of an unclear task, it must not call the task solved and ends with `STATUS: NOT DONE`. (In pilot 1 it said DONE right after writing that its reading was a guess.)

The author notes that change 1 is stricter than how they use the prompt day to day, where the planner may make tiny fixes itself.
